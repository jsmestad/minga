01 Visual Grammar

Two marks carry authorship and state across both clients.

Every color maps to a semantic theme role (minga, you, needs_you, failure, done), defaulting to Catppuccin Frappé. Today's rolling build already ships staged overlays, three-way merge, and syntax-highlighted read-only diff review (Lesson 01).

The Twist (code gutter only)

Authorship & Undo

Braided strands mark Minga's unreviewed lines and pin the crossing to the top of the viewport as you scroll. Edit inside Minga's block and one strand turns blue (you) on the exact lines you touched. Pressing u or ⌘Z undoes only your lines; Minga's steps leave cleanly only through Reject (SPC a d).

The Orbit (six state words, five shapes)

Zero focus theft

One canonical BEAM state renders identically across the modeline, *minga-tasks*, Subagent Peek, and notifications: ◐ Working, ◉ Needs you, ○ Waiting / Stopped, ◆ Failed, and ● Done. Only Needs you escalates; nothing ever steals cursor focus.

02 Workflows

Review inline or in buffers, inspect workers in place, queue prompts like code.

Every agent surface is a real buffer closing with q, never a permanent sidebar fighting your editor for width.

  • ]h / [h
    SPC a y / SPC a d     Inline review while coding. Jump changes with ]h/[h, select a change with ih/ah (e.g. vah SPC a y), repeat with ., apply ex ranges (:'<,'>Minga keep), or press g. for the Act Menu. Keeping a change with an LSP error asks for confirmation first.
  • SPC a r
    *minga-review*     Batch review drawer when Minga finishes. Bare single letters act directly: y keep, n reject, = toggle inline diff, Y keep all (with y/n confirm), ↩ jump to file, q close. :Minga review also populates standard quickfix (]q, :cdo).
  • SPC a a
    *minga-tasks*     Tasks and subagent trees, needs-you first. Uniform approval keys (y once, a session, t turn, n deny, = diff) with a 300 ms anti-misfire lockout on auto-open and Already answered sync across notifications. Press c new task, r retry, o retained output (]]/[[ failures, f fix), i inspect, x stop.
  • SPC a i
    Subagent Peek     Inspect a worker in a right split without leaving your parent thread. Unfold its Brief with za, step siblings in place with ]] / [[, message that worker directly, and keep any unsent draft in your parent composer intact.
  • SPC a v / SPC a q q
    *minga-queue*     Steer mid-flight or edit your queue. In the composer, ↩ in Insert inserts a newline; Normal ↩ or ⌘↩ steers while Minga works; ⌥↩ queues follow-ups into *minga-queue* (reorder with dd/p); ⌘. or SPC a s stops. Attach context via SPC a x b, SPC a x f, and SPC a x d. Watch live edits with SPC a w or publish/discard via SPC a p.

Safe quit (SPC q q)

Quitting with unsaved work lists what is open and prompts with explicit keys: s save all and quit, d discard only your unsaved edits (Minga's unreviewed changes stay saved for next time), or esc cancel.

Voice strictly at the edges

Minga is Bavarian for Munich, kept quiet and strictly at three moments: "Servus." on welcome, "Passt." once when a task completes, and "Ciao." on quit. Everywhere else, status uses plain operational words.
