Learn / Lesson 02 of 04 · The same file

You and Minga, in the same file

You are still editing your spec, and Minga has just made two changes to it. Before you accept anything, you need to know which lines are yours, which are Minga's, and which you both wrote. Minga marks that in the margin of every changed line, and your undo never touches Minga's work. Try it in the file below: it takes four steps.

01 What you saw

The margin answers one question: who wrote this line, and is it settled?

You did not have to read a diff or leave the file. Three marks carried the whole review.

  • One thin line is yours. Every line you wrote and have not committed carries it. If Minga's change removes one of your lines, the change stays marked until you keep it, and rejecting it brings the line back.
  • A double line is Minga's, and it waits for you. The two lines cross on the first line of each change, so you can see where a change starts. A change stays marked until you keep it or reject it.
  • When you edit one of Minga's lines, half of its double line takes your colour. You both wrote that line, and the mark says so.
  • u takes back only what you typed. It never removes Minga's lines. Those leave one way: you reject the change with SPC a d.
  • A kept change goes quiet. SPC a y turns the double line into one faint line, which stays until your next commit.

What you can do differently: let Minga work in a file while you are still editing it. You do not need to stop and wait, because your undo cannot damage Minga's work, and a rejected change cannot take your lines with it.

02 Beyond one file

Two more safeguards, and where the keys come from.

  • When Minga splits a task among helper agents, called subagents, press SPC a i or click a Started row to open that subagent beside the conversation. The subagent peek shows its brief, work and report without leaving the parent conversation.
  • When a list of requests opens on its own, Minga ignores single-letter answers for a moment, so a letter you were already typing cannot approve something: see Drawers.
  • Every key in this lesson comes from the v0.1.0 key plan. It is the plan for v0.1.0, not the keys of today's build.
