01 Workflows

Review inline or in buffers, inspect workers in place, queue prompts like code.

Every agent surface opens as a buffer in a drawer or split and closes with q, trading docked side panels for unobstructed code width.

  • ]h / [h
    SPC a y / SPC a d     Inline review while coding. Jump with ]h/[h, select with ih/ah, apply line ranges (:'<,'>Minga keep), or press g. for the Act Menu. Keeping a change with an LSP error prompts first.
  • SPC a r
    *minga-review*     Batch review drawer. Bare keys act directly: y keep, n reject, = diff, Y keep all (with confirm), q close. :Minga review also fills quickfix.
  • SPC a a
    *minga-tasks*     Tasks and subagent trees, needs-you first. Uniform approval keys (y/a/t/n/=) with a 300 ms lockout on auto-open and Already answered sync everywhere (full keymap).
  • SPC a i
    Subagent Peek     Inspect a worker in a right split. Unfold its brief with za, step siblings with ]]/[[, and message it directly while keeping your parent draft intact.
  • SPC a v / SPC a q q
    *minga-queue*     Steer mid-flight or edit your queue. Normal ↩ or ⌘↩ steers while Minga works; ⌥↩ queues follow-ups into *minga-queue* (reorder with dd/p); SPC a s stops.
Safe quit (SPC q q)

Quitting with unsaved work lists open files and prompts with explicit keys: s save all, d discard only your unsaved edits (Minga's unreviewed changes stay saved), or esc cancel.
