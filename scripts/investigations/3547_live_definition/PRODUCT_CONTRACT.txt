An ordinary Save attempts one immutable source-unit snapshot, not an extra Apply command. Grouped saves specify membership and begin/complete/abort ownership; completion attempts exactly one snapshot. Interrupted groups stay visibly pending or aborted, never inferred complete by elapsed time. Source-save and activation outcomes are separate. A revocable session grant covers source execution during preparation, including compilation, as well as installation; authority is checked before preparation and before installation/publication without prompting for each revision within scope. Revocation closes new admission without undoing prior effects. Pin this written contract before B0/L0 implementation; R0 does not implement its filesystem/UI path.
