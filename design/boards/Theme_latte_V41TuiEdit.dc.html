What renders what

Gutter number, sign, authorship: three fixed columns in one lipgloss.JoinHorizontal. E and W signs bold. The twist is one cell with two strands as foreground and background colour (▐, light over shade); the first line swaps sides with a quadrant block (▚), so the strands cross. Blue thin strand for you, faint when kept. On Kitty, Ghostty, WezTerm and iTerm2 the gutter can draw the exact twist with the graphics protocol instead.
Diagnostics Lip Gloss v2 UnderlineStyle(Curly) + UnderlineColor(Red) for errors (SGR 4:3, 58:2); UnderlineStyle(Dotted) in sand for warnings (line 244). Plain underline where the terminal lacks styled underlines.
Cursor line Surface tint across the row; its diagnostic as faint italic text after the code. Other lines stay clean.
Minga chip reverse video: purple background, dark text, Padding(0,1).
Which-key floats over the code as a layer (lipgloss.NewLayer on the Canvas, already used in go/tui) with a soft drop shadow: a dim ▌ edge on the right and ▀ edge below. One lipgloss.RoundedBorder() panel, BorderForeground(PopupBorder), Background(PopupSurface). Title and live counts in the top border, help in the bottom border. Rows single-spaced, columns sized to their longest entry with a 6-cell gap, group names dim above each column, no rules. The panel is only as tall as its longest group.
Working spinner the ◐ in the modeline and which-key header is a bubbles/spinner with Frames: ◐ ◓ ◑ ◒ at 8 fps; static ◐ when motion is reduced.
Modeline segments are styled blocks joined with one-cell gaps: mode pill (ModeNormal), needs-you chip, file, change, git, Minga, E/W chips, filetype, position. No powerline arrows.
Degradation colorprofile maps tints to 256 and ANSI 16; shapes and words carry meaning when colour collapses.
