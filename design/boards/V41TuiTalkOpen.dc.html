What renders what

Conversation a bordered viewport on Surface: speaker labels in their colour, plain prose, tool steps as one line with a state glyph. No bubbles, no rails.
Queue a small bordered buffer on SurfaceAlt, editable with dd and p; its keys sit in its border.
Composer bubbles/textarea in a RoundedBorder that turns the focus colour while focused. Hints live in its bottom border and only while focused. ⌘. has no terminal key, so stop is SPC a s.
Links file names and file:line in tool steps are OSC 8 hyperlinks (Lip Gloss v2 Hyperlink).
Find file floats as a layer over the dimmed editor with a soft half-block drop shadow (lipgloss.NewLayer / Canvas, already used in go/tui). bubbles/textinput prompt, results as a sectioned list, matched characters bold and underlined in your blue, full-width selection bar.
Editor behind dimmed while a drawer has focus.
