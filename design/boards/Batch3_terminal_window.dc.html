Exits

⌃\ ⌃N  →  terminal.drawer.normal (Normal mode on this terminal's scrollback)
SPC ,  →  files.switcher (terminals listed under Terminals with cwd and state)
:q  →  edit.plain (asks to kill the shell only if a job is running)
exit  →  terminal.exited
SPC o T always makes a new terminal (*terminal 2*); SPC o t keeps reusing the drawer's terminal. The switcher shows both, so you never lose one.
